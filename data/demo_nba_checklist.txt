SAMPLE NBA COURSE FILE CHECKLIST — ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY
This is a demonstration checklist, not an official NBA interpretation or exhaustive list.

A course file may include:
- Approved course syllabus and course information.
- Course outcomes and CO-PO/PSO mapping, as applicable.
- Course delivery plan and teaching-learning activities.
- Assessment question papers, assignments and rubrics.
- Attainment methodology, calculations and results.
- Analysis of attainment and documented improvement actions.
- Supporting evidence for implementation.

Always map evidence to the current applicable NBA manual and institution-approved process. Verify criteria and formats against official current documents.
